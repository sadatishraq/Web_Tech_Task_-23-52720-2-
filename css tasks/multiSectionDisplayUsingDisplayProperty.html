The humanitarian crisis in Gaza continues to draw international concern as the ongoing Israel-Hamas war has resulted in widespread destruction, large-scale displacement, and significant civilian casualties. Many countries, humanitarian organizations, and legal experts have called for an immediate ceasefire and increased humanitarian aid. Some governments, the United Nations, and human rights organizations have described Israel's actions as genocide, while Israel strongly rejects that characterization. The conflict remains the subject of ongoing international legal proceedings and global debate.
